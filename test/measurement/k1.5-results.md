Runs of 2026-11-06 for K1.5 of the guide-tool epic (`stateui-guide-tool.md`), phase `after-guide` (logs, transcripts and reviews under `.measurements/after-guide/`).

- **Commits:** stateui-mcp `4a7a311` with the K1.5 changes uncommitted (the tool-call log's instructions line and the summary script; the tools, the instructions and the topics are those of `4a7a311`), stateui-app `7a51b0b`, stateui-components `de69106`; `npm run mcp:build` at the workspace root before the runs, so the runtime bundle was rebuilt (`705662b46bf60fb7.js`). Presets: yes.
- **Model, harness, prompts and reviewer:** as S1.2 and the "After the epic 2 fixes" rows: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`) in `claude -p`, one fresh session per build in an empty folder outside the repository, `--strict-mcp-config` with its own stdio stateui-mcp (fresh `--data-dir`, `--tool-log`), `--tools ""`, `--no-session-persistence`; three builds at a time on ports 4300, 4310 and 4320 (`scripts/measurement/runBuild.mjs`); the prompts of `requests.md` word for word. - **How the numbers are counted** (`scripts/summarizeToolLog.mjs`): instructions tokens are the length of the served instructions (5,157 characters, logged once per session) divided by four, counted once per build; catalog tokens are the response characters of the catalog tools divided by four; guide tokens those of `ui_guide`; tokens read are instructions plus catalog plus guide. The table gives the catalog tools apart from the guide, so the catalog column compares with the earlier rows.
- **The "before" figures:** instructions 15,669 characters (commit `10a0343`, K1.2's figure), 3,917 tokens, plus the catalog tokens of the "After the epic 2 fixes" runs (there was no `ui_guide` then): store 3,917 + 10,595 = 14,512, finance 3,917 + 9,314 = 13,231, booking 3,917 + 9,935 = 13,852. ## Gate verdict

The gate: the median of instructions plus catalog plus guide tokens read is lower, and the median checklist items met is not lower than the "After the epic 2 fixes" medians (store 11, finance 10, booking 9).

| Request | Tokens read, before → after | Lower? | Checklist met, before → after | Not lower? | Verdict |

| Store | 14512 → 15649 (+8 %) | no | 11 → 11 | yes | **fail** (tokens) |
| Finance dashboard | 13231 → 14971 (+13 %) | no | 10 → 10 | yes | **fail** (tokens) |
| Booking app | 13852 → 13593 (−2 %) | yes | 9 → 9 | yes | **pass** |
| Floor plan | none → 10356 | first row | none → 10 of 11 | first row | recorded, no comparison |

**Overall: fail for store and finance on tokens read; build quality held** (every checklist median is equal, at the maximum for store and finance).

**Why tokens read did not drop.** The instructions save 2,628 tokens per build (3,917 to 1,289), and the agents read about 2,900 tokens of guide per build (5 to 9 `ui_guide` calls). The largest single guide read is the topic `start`: 11 of the 12 builds read it (5,250 characters, about 1,313 tokens), and it repeats the server instructions the client already showed (its pointer line says to read it "when your client does not show the server instructions"). Without those reads, guide tokens would be about 1,600 per build and the instructions plus guide about 2,900 against 3,917 before. The rest of the gap is the catalog tools: store and finance read 8 % and 10 % more of them than in the earlier runs, within the spread of earlier phases (finance ranged 8,085 to 13,174 across phases). Following the epic's technical note, the first fix is the pointer wording of K1.2 (for example, the `start` topic named only for a client without the instructions, or answering a short note when the instructions were shown), not putting the text back.

Builds that read each topic (of 12): `logic` 12, `paths` 12, `start` 11, `modules` 10, `live-view` 10, `parts` 9, `sections` 9, `drawing` 3 (every floor plan), `keys` 3, `safety` 2, the index 4. No build read `revisions`, `files` or the paint-field and 3D topics.

## Failed writes and their cause

Counted from the tool-call log (server-side failures; error codes of the refusals from the transcripts).

- **Store** (13, 11, 24): `invalid-app` refusals of `ui_logic_write` (21) and `ui_nodes_write` (19), `ui_screen_create` (6). Causes: logic schema (`SUI-LOGIC-SHAPE` 17, `SUI-LOGIC-PATH` 3, `SUI-LOGIC-EXPRESSION`), links to state that does not exist yet (`SUI-LINK-MISSING` 9, `SUI-LINK-TARGET` 3), `on` targets (`SUI-ON-TARGET` 9); store 3 had 18 `SUI-STATE-LABEL` errors on one screen, which is its high first-write count (51).
- **Finance** (10, 14, 7): `ui_logic_write` 16 (`SUI-LOGIC-SHAPE` 12, `SUI-LOGIC-EXPRESSION` 3), `ui_nodes_write` 8 (`SUI-ON-TARGET`, `SUI-STATE-UNKNOWN`, `SUI-LINK-MISSING`), `ui_state_set` refused with `shared-dataset` 2, one `conflict` (stale revision), one `invalid-records` data write. No logic cascade this time: first-write errors fell from 26 to 9.
- **Booking** (9, 17, 10): `ui_logic_write` 16 (`SUI-LOGIC-SHAPE` 12), `ui_nodes_write` 8, `ui_screen_create` 6 (`SUI-LINK-MISSING` 9, `SUI-DOC-PATH` 3, `SUI-DOC-ORDER` 3), `ui_state_set` `shared-dataset` 3.
- **Floor plan** (4, 9, 10): `ui_nodes_write` 13 (`SUI-ON-SHAPE` 4, layout keys `SUI-LAYOUT-TYPE`/`-SETTING`/`-VALUE`/`-UNSUPPORTED` 8, `SUI-ON-TARGET` 3, one `SUI-STATE-PAINT`), `ui_logic_write` 6 (`SUI-LOGIC-SHAPE` 6), `ui_model_write` 2 (`SUI-MODEL-SHAPE`), 2 `conflict`. Drawing 1 also had one `ui_nodes_write` refused by the client's own schema check before it reached the server (not in the log). None of the floor-plan failures was about drawing shapes or their links.

The logic schema (`SUI-LOGIC-SHAPE`) stays the most common cause in every request, as in every earlier phase. Failed writes are lower than after the epic 2 fixes for store (19 to 13) and finance (16 to 10) and one higher for booking (9 to 10).

## Checklist misses

- **Store, finance:** none; every build met every item.
- **Booking:** a cancelled booking stays in the list with a "cancelled" badge, behind a confirm dialog (all three builds, item 10); booking 1 drew no room pictures (item 3); booking 2 let a reversed date range through to a confirmed booking (item 1), had no step indicator (item 8) and truncated the owner table's dates (item 9).
- **Floor plan:** every build missed only item 11: at 390 px the plan fits, nothing scrolls sideways and the panel moves below, but the room names on the scaled-down drawing are about 7 to 9 px, which the reviewer found too small to read. Every build drew six rooms in a drawing with their names, free and booked colours from the records, a pressable room that sets the selection, a panel that books and frees, and a drawn gauge linked to the free count.
